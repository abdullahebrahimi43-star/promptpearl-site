A cartoon episode in four steps

  1. Create characters — describe them or design them with AI. They look the same in every scene.
  2. Write the story — type an idea, pick the length (from 40 seconds to about 10 minutes) and the language.
  3. Draw and voice — draw all scenes at once, pick a voice for each character, animate the scenes you like.
  4. Make the episode — music, subtitles and voices are joined into one video, in 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for Shorts and Reels.

Questions

Who can use PromptPearl AI?

Adults 18 and older. Many of our users make content for their own YouTube channels, including channels for children.

Is my content safe and private?

Your creations are stored privately and only you can see them unless you share them. Every prompt and result is checked automatically for safety. See the Privacy Policy and Content Policy.

Can I use what I make on social media?

Yes — post your pictures, videos and episodes on your own channels. Please follow our Terms and each platform's rules.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or see how to delete your account.
