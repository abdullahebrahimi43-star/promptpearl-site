Content Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · PrimeEdge Solutions LLC

PromptPearl is for creative, positive work: art, stories, cartoons for kids' channels, products, social posts. Every prompt, upload and generated picture is checked automatically, and blocked requests do not use credits.

Never allowed

  • Any sexual or suggestive content involving minors, or anything that sexualizes children in any way. We report this to the authorities.
  • Sexual or nude content of any kind.
  • Pictures or videos of a real person made to embarrass, deceive or harm them, intimate images of anyone without consent, or uploading other people's photos without their permission.
  • Deceptive content: fake news, fake documents, impersonating real people or organizations, or election misinformation.
  • Violence, gore, self-harm, terrorism, weapons instructions or content that encourages harm.
  • Hate or harassment targeting people for who they are.
  • Content that copies trademarks, logos, or copyrighted characters in a way that infringes others' rights.
  • Anything illegal where you live or in the United States.

Content made for children's channels

You can make cartoons and stories for young audiences. Keep them age-appropriate, and follow the rules of the platform where you publish (for example YouTube's "made for kids" requirements).

How reports work

  1. Tap the flag on any creation or story, or email promptpearl.support@gmail.com.
  2. Our team reviews reports, usually within 2 business days, and removes content that breaks this policy.
  3. Accounts that break this policy may be limited, suspended or closed. Serious cases are reported to the authorities.

AI limits

Automatic checks are not perfect. If something gets through that should not have, please report it.
