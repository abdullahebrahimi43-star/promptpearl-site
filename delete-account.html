Delete your PromptPearl AI account

PromptPearl AI by PrimeEdge Solutions LLC

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PromptPearl AI and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email promptpearl.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my PromptPearl account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in
  • All your pictures, videos, uploaded photos, characters, stories, voices, episodes and post packs
  • Your credit balance, credit history and purchase records in our database

What is kept

  • Automatic safety-check records, without any link to your account
  • A minimal note that an account was deleted, for security
  • Purchase records held by Google Play or Apple under their own policies

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in the store; see Subscriptions & Refunds.

Want a copy first? In the app: Profile → Export my data.
