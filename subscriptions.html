Long cartoon stories

  • Cartoon stories of up to 8 scenes: voices are free.
  • Stories of 12 scenes or more: voices use 1 credit per scene, in addition to drawing (1 credit per scene) and any animation.
  • Stories of 20 scenes or more (about 3–10 minute episodes) are part of the paid plans.

How renewal works

  • Plans renew automatically every month until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Google Play or Apple account at purchase and at each renewal.
  • Each renewal gives you that month's plan credits. Unused plan credits end when the month ends.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. After cancelling, your plan and its credits stay until the end of the period you paid for.

How to cancel

  • Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → PromptPearl AI Studio → Cancel. Or in the app: Credits → your plan → Manage.
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PromptPearl AI Studio → Cancel.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in the store too.

Refunds

  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple, so refunds are handled under their policies: Google Play refunds · Apple refunds.
  • If a creation fails, its credits are returned to you automatically.
  • If something went wrong with a purchase or credits, contact support@promptpearl.app or use Help & support in the app and we will make it right where we can.
  • Credits from a purchase that is refunded may be removed.
